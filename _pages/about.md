Mijoo Kim is an AI Research Scientist at [VUNO Inc.](https://www.vuno.co/), where she focuses on advancing robust and trustworthy medical AI — from 3D brain MRI and chest X-ray analysis to the data pipelines and MLOps infrastructure that carry research into products.

Before joining VUNO, she was a Graduate Research Assistant at the [Computer Vision and Machine Learning Lab](https://sites.google.com/view/cau-cvml/) at [Chung-Ang University](https://www.cau.ac.kr/), where she earned her M.S. in Artificial Intelligence under Professor [Junseok Kwon](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=lwsaTnEAAAAJ), and a visiting scholar at [Carnegie Mellon University](https://www.cmu.edu/), where she worked with [Zhi-Qi Cheng](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=uB2He2UAAAAJ) and Professor [Alexander Hauptmann](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=Py54GcEAAAAJ).

During her graduate studies, she conducted research on uncertainty calibration and distribution shift robustness, extending to the robust fine-tuning of vision-language models, with publications at top-tier CV/ML conferences, e.g. [`ECCV`](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.12330) and [`NeurIPS`](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.01723).

Her research interests center on computer vision and deep learning, with a particular emphasis on `uncertainty calibration`, `trustworthy AI`, and `vision-language models`, and she aims to further develop practical and reliable AI techniques that can be applied across diverse domains.
