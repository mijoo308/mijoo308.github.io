Mijoo Kim

Applied AI Research Scientist @ VUNO Inc.

VUNO Inc.

Seoul, Korea

Mijoo Kim is an AI Research Scientist at VUNO Inc., where she focuses on advancing robust and trustworthy medical AI — from 3D brain MRI and chest X-ray analysis to the data pipelines and MLOps infrastructure that carry research into products.

Before joining VUNO, she was a Graduate Research Assistant at the Computer Vision and Machine Learning Lab at Chung-Ang University, where she earned her M.S. in Artificial Intelligence under Professor Junseok Kwon, and a visiting scholar at Carnegie Mellon University, where she worked with Zhi-Qi Cheng and Professor Alexander Hauptmann.

During her graduate studies, she conducted research on uncertainty calibration and distribution shift robustness, extending to the robust fine-tuning of vision-language models, with publications at top-tier CV/ML conferences, e.g. ECCV and NeurIPS.

Her research interests center on computer vision and deep learning, with a particular emphasis on uncertainty calibration, trustworthy AI, and vision-language models, and she aims to further develop practical and reliable AI techniques that can be applied across diverse domains.
