While working on OCR projects as an undergraduate researcher, I contributed to [EasyOCR](https://github.com/JaidedAI/EasyOCR), one of the most widely used open-source OCR frameworks (28k+ stars):

- Implemented the **rotated-text recognition option** (`rotation_info`), a frequently requested feature — merged and still available as a public API option today
- Fixed a **post-processing bug** affecting recognition output

Small PRs, but they taught me how to read an unfamiliar codebase, follow a maintainer's conventions, and ship changes that thousands of users depend on.
