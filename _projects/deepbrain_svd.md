At VUNO, I developed a **small vessel disease (SVD) localization model** using cross-modality MRI registration, and carried it end-to-end from research prototype to a **new feature in VUNO Med®-DeepBrain®**, a regulatory-cleared brain MRI analysis product. An abstract based on this work was submitted to RSNA 2025.

Related research threads:

- MRI-based automated diagnosis systems, in collaboration with a major academic medical center for clinical validation
- Longitudinal MRI segmentation with explicit modeling of inter-scan registration and temporal changes
- Clinical report generation using small LLMs (e.g., gpt-oss, MedGemma) conditioned on imaging model outputs
