As an undergraduate researcher at the Visual Computing Lab (JBNU), I worked on **digitizing industrial piping & instrumentation diagrams (P&IDs)**: detecting and recognizing symbols and text in extremely dense engineering drawings at an industrially applicable level.

I was responsible for the **text detection** component and built a **PyQt GUI** for visualizing and correcting recognition results.

- **Paper**: [Deep-learning-based recognition of symbols and texts at an industrially applicable level from images of high-density piping and instrumentation diagrams](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.eswa.2021.115337), Expert Systems with Applications (2021)
- A follow-up approach using synthetic data and text-symbol integrated detection was published in the [Korean Journal of Computational Design and Engineering](https://doi.org/10.7315/CDE.2021.355) (2021)
