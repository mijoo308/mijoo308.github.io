A hackathon project (JBNU Idea Hackathon 2020, **Excellence Prize**) proposing policies, services, and an app for the smooth utilization of pregnancy seats in subways.

- Idea conceptualization and development
- Android app development, with an Arduino-based seat indicator (LED/LCD) prototype
