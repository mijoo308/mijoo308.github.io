During my computer vision internship at NextLab, I automated a **highway drone control pipeline**: detecting illegal vehicles driving in the first lane, in real time, from live drone video.

- Combined **lane region recognition, vehicle type classification, and optical-flow-based tracking** into a single pipeline
- Successfully **demonstrated on a live highway in Cheonan**
- The technology was filed as a **Korean patent** (10-2022-0143599, "AI-Based Road Control System and Method Using Drones")

During the same internship I also built a subtitle anomaly detection system and an end-to-end Korean OCR pipeline — see the separate project pages.
