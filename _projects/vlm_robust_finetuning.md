Fine-tuning vision-language models like CLIP improves downstream accuracy but often destroys their robustness and calibration on out-of-distribution data. This work proposes a **robust fine-tuning framework that improves both accuracy and calibration under distribution shifts**.

Conducted during my visiting scholarship at Carnegie Mellon University; first presented at the NeurIPS 2023 Workshop on Distribution Shifts and published at NeurIPS 2024.

- **Paper**: [Towards Calibrated Robust Fine-Tuning of Vision-Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.01723), NeurIPS 2024
